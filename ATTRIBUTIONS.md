## Persian typefaces

Both Persian families are commercial fonts licensed to ChangeMoment Mental Health Centre
and self-hosted from `public/fonts`. Keep these licence codes with the repository — they
are the proof of purchase for the webfont files committed here.

| Family | Role | Files | Licence code |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Doran 3 (Eco) | Persian headings (`--font-fa-heading`) | `Doran-Regular.woff2`, `Doran-ExtraBold.woff2` | `1UX6A0BI` |
| Pinar V4 | Persian body text (`--font-fa-body`) | `Pinar-{Light,Regular,Medium,SemiBold,Bold,ExtraBold,Black}.woff2` | `YTU7LVP6QP` |

The same codes are recorded in the header comment of `src/styles/fonts.css`. They are
deliberately not emitted into the served HTML.
