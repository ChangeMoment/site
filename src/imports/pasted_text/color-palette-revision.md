Revise the visual color system across the entire ChangeMoment website.

The current design feels too dominated by beige, cream, nude, and warm brown tones. It is starting to look too close to common bohemian / neutral wellness website designs. I like the warmth and softness, but the website must not feel overly nude, beige, boho, or generic.

The brand should still feel calm, bright, premium, soft, and welcoming, but the green side of the palette must become more visible and more intentional across all pages.

Important:
Do not remove the cream / bone tones from the palette. They are still part of the brand. But they should no longer dominate the entire website.

Use the green palette more actively:

* Deep Olive: #7C8067
* Dry Sage: #C0C79E
* Muted Olive: #999A72

Keep the existing warm tones, but reduce their dominance:

* Faded Copper: #B18369
* Desert Sand: #DDBDA6
* Bone: #E8DDD0
* White: #FFFFFF

New color direction:

* White should remain the main background color.
* Bone and Desert Sand should be used as soft supporting backgrounds, not as the dominant visual identity.
* Dry Sage #C0C79E should become a clearly visible brand accent across the website.
* Muted Olive #999A72 should be used for section accents, cards, subtle highlights, icon backgrounds, dividers, hover states, and visual rhythm.
* Deep Olive #7C8067 can be used in selected important places where stronger contrast and a more grounded brand presence is needed.
* Use Faded Copper #B18369 more selectively, mainly for warm CTAs or small emotional accents, not as the main repeated color everywhere.

The goal is to make the website feel:

* calm
* bright
* open
* premium
* natural
* trustworthy
* fresh
* less bohemian
* less nude/beige-heavy
* more connected to the green ChangeMoment identity

Apply this correction across all pages:

* Home
* About Us
* Our Team
* Our Services
* Service Detail pages
* Blogs
* Contact
* Book an Appointment

Specific changes to make:

1. Reduce large beige blocks
   Where large sections currently use Bone / beige backgrounds, alternate them with:

* white backgrounds
* very soft sage backgrounds
* white cards on sage-tinted sections
* sage accents and dividers

Do not stack too many beige sections after each other.

2. Add more green visual rhythm
   Use Dry Sage and Muted Olive in:

* service card icon backgrounds
* section labels / eyebrows
* small decorative lines
* hover states
* selected badges
* feature icons
* step numbers
* FAQ active states
* language switcher active state
* subtle background shapes
* blog category tags
* service detail visual accents

3. Make CTA colors more balanced
   Primary CTA can still use Faded Copper or warm brown where appropriate, but create more variation:

* Primary warm CTA: #B18369
* Secondary olive CTA: #7C8067
* Light CTA background: #C0C79E with dark text where contrast allows
* Outline CTA: olive border with white or transparent background

Use olive CTA styles in some strategic sections so the site does not feel all warm brown.

4. Improve the Home page color balance
   The Home page currently feels too beige-heavy.
   Update it so:

* Hero stays bright and mostly white.
* “Why people choose ChangeMoment” should not feel like a full nude block; use a softer white/sage composition.
* “How we can support you” should include more visible sage/olive accents in service cards.
* “How it works” can use a sage-tinted section instead of a beige-heavy section.
* Team preview can use Bone softly, but add olive details.
* Blog cards should use white with sage tags and olive hover states.
* Final CTA can use a balanced Bone + Sage composition, not only beige.

5. Keep the brand premium
   Do not make the green too saturated or medical.
   Avoid hospital green, neon green, mint green, and bright lime.
   The green should feel like refined olive / dry sage / calm natural green.

6. Avoid generic wellness look
   Do not turn the site into:

* boho beige wellness
* spa website
* yoga studio
* earthy handmade brand
* coffee / lifestyle brand

The result should feel like a premium mental health centre in Canada with a calm, professional, emotionally safe identity.

7. Preserve accessibility
   Maintain strong contrast for all text and buttons.
   Use Deep Olive #7C8067 only where text contrast works well.
   Do not place low-contrast beige text on beige backgrounds.
   All buttons, links, and form states must remain readable and accessible.

8. Preserve layout and content
   Do not rewrite the site architecture.
   Do not remove pages.
   Do not remove sections.
   Do not remove CTAs.
   Only improve the color system, visual balance, and brand feeling.

Final visual target:
The website should still feel soft and warm, but now the green identity should be clearly present. The first impression should be calm, fresh, open, professional, and emotionally safe — not beige bohemian.
