Online counselling:

1.	Is online counselling as effective as in-person counselling?
Yes. Research consistently shows online therapy can be just as effective as in-person therapy. It allows you to receive support from a comfortable and private space.
2.	Is my session private and secure?
Yes. Your privacy is very important to us. Sessions are held through a secure online platform, and your personal information is kept confidential.
3.	How does an online session work?
After booking your appointment, you will receive a private link to join your session. At the scheduled time, you simply click the link and meet with your counsellor online.
4.	What do I need to join?
A quiet, private space, a stable internet connection, and a device with a camera. That’s it.
5.	What if I’m not comfortable with technology?
That is completely okay. The platform is very easy to use and will guide you step by step so you feel comfortable joining your session.
6.	Can I do therapy on my phone?
Yes. You can join your online counselling session from your phone, as long as you have a stable internet connection and a private space.
7.	Can couples or families do therapy together online?
Yes. Couples and family members can join online sessions together, even if they are in different locations.
8.	What if I’m not sure this service is right for me?
You are welcome to book a free consultation. We can talk about your needs and help you decide whether online counselling feels like a good fit.
9.	Is online counselling covered by insurance?
Yes, if you have coverage for in-person counselling, most insurance providers will provide coverage for online therapy. Coverage depends on your insurance provider, and we provide receipts that you can submit for reimbursement. Please check with your insurance provider to verify your coverage.


Individual Counselling:



1.	How do I know if I’m ready for individual counselling?
There’s no “perfect” time to start therapy. If you’re feeling drawn to exploring therapy, that’s often reason enough to reach out.You do not need to have everything figured out before starting. If something feels heavy, confusing, painful, or hard to manage alone, counselling can be a helpful place to begin.
2.	What if I don’t know where to start?
That is very common. Your counsellor will help you slow down, explore what has been happening, and find a starting point together.
3.	How do I choose the right therapist?
Beyond credentials, the right therapist is someone you feel safe, respected, and comfortable with. A free consultation can help you get a sense of whether the connection feels right.Trust your instincts about who feels like a good match for your healing journey. 
4.	What is the difference between counselling and therapy?
The terms are often used in similar ways.both can support healing, self-understanding, and personal growth. What matters most is finding a counsellor that feel right for you and your needs.
5.	What approaches do you use?
At ChangeMoment Mental Health Center, we use an evidence-based and personalized approach based on your needs, emotions, relationships, and life experiences.
6.	Can individual counselling be done online?
Yes. Individual counselling can be offered online, so you can attend sessions from a private and comfortable space.
7.	How often should I attend individual counselling?
Many clients start with weekly sessions to build momentum. The frequency can be adjusted based on your needs, goals, and availability.
8.	How long does individual counselling take?
There is no fixed timeline. Some people come for a few focused sessions, while others continue longer to work through deeper patterns and experiences.
9.	What if I’m not sure this service is right for me?
You are welcome to book a free consultation. We can talk briefly about what you are looking for and see whether individual counselling is the right next step.
10.	Is individual counselling covered by insurance?
Many extended health plans cover counselling with a Registered Clinical Counsellor. Coverage depends on your insurance provider, and we provide receipts you can submit for reimbursement. Please check with your insurance provider to verify your coverage.


Couples Counselling:



1.	When should couples seek couples counselling?
Couples counselling can be helpful when you feel stuck in repeated conflicts, emotional distance, trust issues, communication problems, or major life transitions. You do not have to wait until things feel too difficult.
2.	What can we expect during our first session of couples counselling?
In the first session, your counsellor will get to know both of you, understand your concerns, and explore what brings you to counselling. It is a space to slow down and begin understanding the patterns between you.
3.	What if we are unsure about staying together?
That uncertainty is welcome here. Couples counselling can help you explore your relationship with more clarity, honesty, and care, whether your goal is to rebuild the relationship or better understand what each of you needs.
4.	Do we have to attend all sessions together?
Most sessions are attended together, but sometimes individual sessions may be recommended as part of the process. This will be discussed clearly with both partners.
5.	Can you help with high-conflict co-parenting?
Yes, counselling can support communication, boundaries, and emotional regulation in co-parenting relationships. However, we do not provide legal advice, custody recommendations, or formal parenting assessments.
6.	Will the therapist take sides?
No. The therapist’s role is not to blame or choose sides,Your counsellor stays neutral and works for the relationship, helping both of you feel heard, respected, and understood.
7.	What if we can’t agree on a therapist?
It is important that both partners feel comfortable with the therapist. A free consultation can help you both ask questions and see whether the fit feels right.
8.	How often do we need to attend sessions?
Many couples begin with weekly or biweekly sessions. The frequency can be adjusted based on your needs, goals, progress and availability.
9.	How long does couples counselling take?
There is no fixed timeline. Some couples come for short-term support, while others need more time to work through deeper patterns, wounds, or trust concerns.
10.	Can we do couples counselling online?
Yes. Couples counselling can be offered online, and partners can join from the same location or different locations.
11.	Do you provide legal or financial advice?
No. Couples counselling is not a substitute for legal or financial advice. We can support emotional communication and decision-making, but legal or financial questions should be discussed with the appropriate professionals. We can support you stay grounded while managing those aspects.
12.	What if we’re not sure this service is right for us?
You are welcome to book a free consultation. We can briefly talk about your situation and help you decide whether couples counselling is a good place to start.
13.	Is couples counselling covered by insurance?
Many extended health plans cover counselling with a Registered Clinical Counsellor. Coverage depends on your insurance provider, and we provide receipts you can submit for reimbursement. Please check with your insurance provider to verify your coverage.
