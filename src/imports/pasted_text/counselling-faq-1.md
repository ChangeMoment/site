LGBTQ+ Counselling FAQ:


Is your LGBTQ+ care genuinely affirming?
Definitely!. LGBTQ+ counselling at ChangeMoment Mental Health Center is grounded in respect, safety, and acceptance. You do not need to hide, minimize, or defend who you are in therapy.
Do I have to explain or justify my LGBTQ+ identity?
Not at all! You do not have to prove, explain, or justify your identity. Therapy is a space where your experience is taken seriously, and we meet you with curiosity, care, and respect.
Is LGBTQ+ counselling confidential?
Absolutely! Your privacy is very important. What you share in counselling is kept confidential, within the usual legal and ethical limits of counselling.
Do I need to be out to come to LGBTQ+ therapy?
No. You do not need to be out to family, friends, work, or anyone else to begin therapy. Counselling can support you wherever you are in your process, including questioning, exploring, or privately understanding your identity.
What is gender-affirming care in an LGBTQ+ therapy context?
In therapy, gender-affirming care means supporting and respecting your gender identity without trying to change or “fix” it. It may include exploring identity, emotions, relationships, safety, transition-related stress, family dynamics, or the impact of stigma.
Can LGBTQ+ counselling help with relationship or family issues?
Yes. LGBTQ+ counselling can support you with dating, partnership concerns, family conflict, communication, boundaries, coming out, acceptance, or feeling misunderstood by people close to you.
Do I have to be LGBTQ+ to seek LGBTQ+ counselling?
No. You may seek LGBTQ+ counselling if you are questioning your identity, supporting someone you love, or wanting a space that understands LGBTQ+ experiences and relationships.
Does LGBTQ+ counselling help with bullying?
Yes. Counselling can help you process the emotional impact of bullying, discrimination, rejection, or feeling unsafe. It can also support confidence, boundaries, coping skills, and finding safer support systems.
How often do I need to attend LGBTQ+ counselling sessions?
Many clients begin with weekly sessions. The frequency can be adjusted based on your needs, goals, progress, and availability.
How long does LGBTQ+ counselling take?
There is no fixed timeline. Some people come for short-term or focused support, while others continue longer to explore identity, relationships, family experiences, trauma, self-acceptance, or deeper emotional patterns.
What if I’m not sure LGBTQ+ counselling is right for me?
You are welcome to book a free consultation. We can talk briefly about your needs and help you decide whether this service feels like a good fit.
Is LGBTQ+ counselling covered by insurance?
Many extended health plans cover counselling with a Registered Clinical Counsellor. Coverage depends on your insurance provider, and we provide receipts you can submit for reimbursement. Please check with your insurance provider to confirm your coverage.


CVAP Counselling FAQ : 


What is CVAP?
CVAP stands for the Crime Victim Assistance Program. It is a B.C. government program that may provide benefits, including counselling, to help victims, immediate family members, and some witnesses cope with the effects of violent crime.
Who is eligible for CVAP counselling?
You may be eligible if you were physically or psychologically harmed by a violent crime that happened in British Columbia, or if you are an immediate family member or certain witness affected by the crime. Eligibility is decided by CVAP after you apply.
How do I apply for CVAP funding?
You can complete the CVAP application form and submit it to the program by email, fax, or mail. A local victim service program can also help you with the application process.
Is the care trauma-informed?
Yes. At ChangeMoment Mental Health Center, CVAP counselling is offered with a trauma-informed approach. This means we focus on safety, trust, choice, emotional regulation, and moving at a pace that feels manageable for you.
How many sessions are covered by CVAP?
The number of counselling sessions or hours depends on CVAP’s written decision. If counselling is approved, the decision will usually explain how many hours are funded. If more support is needed later, a reassessment may be requested by the claimant.
Can I choose my counsellor for CVAP counselling?
Yes, you can choose a counsellor who is registered with CVAP. All of our team members are approved to provide this service.
How often do I need to attend CVAP counselling sessions?
Many clients begin with weekly sessions. The frequency can be adjusted based on your needs, goals, progress, availability, and the number of sessions approved by CVAP.
How long does CVAP counselling take?
There is no fixed timeline. Some people come for short-term or focused support after a traumatic event, while others may need longer to process the emotional impact of violence, fear, grief, or safety concerns. The length of counselling also depends on the number of sessions approved by CVAP.
What if I’m not sure CVAP counselling is right for me?
You are welcome to book a free consultation. We can talk briefly about your needs and help you decide whether CVAP counselling feels like a good fit.
Is CVAP counselling covered by insurance?
CVAP counselling is usually funded through the Crime Victim Assistance Program if your application is approved. This is separate from private extended health insurance. If you are using private insurance for any counselling services, please check with your insurance provider to confirm your coverage.

ICBC Counselling FAQ:


Does ICBC cover counselling?
Yes. If you have been injured in a motor vehicle accident in B.C., ICBC may cover counselling as part of your recovery benefits. Under Enhanced Care, counselling is included in the pre-approved treatments available during the first 12 weeks after the crash.
What if I wasn’t physically injured?
You may still benefit from counselling if the accident affected you emotionally or psychologically. Some people experience anxiety, fear of driving, stress, sleep problems, panic, or trauma responses after a crash, even without a major physical injury. It is best to report your claim and speak with ICBC about your situation.
Do I need to contact ICBC directly to get approval for my sessions?
For the first 12 weeks after the accident, ICBC says you do not need a doctor’s referral or special approval to begin pre-approved treatments, including counselling. 
How many ICBC counselling sessions are covered?
During the first 12 weeks after a crash, ICBC lists 12 counselling sessions as pre-approved, with coverage up to ICBC’s approved session rate.
What if I need more counselling than initially approved?
If you need more support after the initial approved sessions or after the first 12 weeks, additional counselling may require approval from ICBC. Your counsellor can usually help explain what information may be needed to request continued support.
How long does it take to get approval for ICBC counselling?
For the first 12 weeks, you can usually begin once you have your ICBC claim number because counselling is pre-approved during that period. If you need additional sessions later, the timeline can vary depending on ICBC’s review and decision.
What if I don’t have a claim number?
You will need to submit a claim to ICBC first. ICBC states that the claim number is created when you submit your claim, and it is needed to access pre-approved treatment.
What types of therapy are available for ICBC counselling?
ICBC counselling can support emotional recovery after a crash, including anxiety, trauma, stress, fear of driving, sleep difficulties, and adjustment after the accident. At ChangeMoment Mental Health Center, we offer trauma-informed counselling that focuses on emotional safety, nervous system regulation, coping tools, and gradual recovery.
How often do I need to attend ICBC counselling sessions?
Many clients begin with weekly sessions. The frequency can be adjusted based on your needs, goals, progress, availability, and ICBC-approved coverage.
How long does ICBC counselling take?
The length of counselling depends on ICBC’s approval and coverage.
What if I’m not sure ICBC counselling is right for me?
You are welcome to book a free consultation. We can talk briefly about your needs and help you decide whether ICBC counselling feels like a good fit.
Is ICBC counselling covered by insurance?
ICBC counselling may be covered through your ICBC claim if you are eligible. This is separate from private extended health insurance.
